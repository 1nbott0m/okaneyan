### Deep shortlist decision

Five sources have bounded jobs in the proposal: Morae #2 for single-focal-object composition; PORTO ROCHA / Robinhood #14 for refined type character (not its font/palette); user-supplied Okaneyan image for brand and material; Sumeria #32 for account-context UX; Codrops #49 for a state transition that preserves a visual anchor. This is five sources total, including the owner-provided identity reference; Motion.dev remains implementation technique corroboration, not one of the selected five. AETHER is studied but not selected. Nimbus is a required contrast/rejection reference, not a positive ingredient. The five are not styles to average. Native motion, full app-runtime states, Nimbus live preview, full resolution of every listing image, 768px/app evidence and independent critique remain unverified.

## Proposed one-screen information order

1. «Обзор» and discreet privacy control.
2. Primary answer: available amount, or honest missing-data state.
3. Immediate explanation: what is included and until which date.
4. A single horizon axis: now → next confirmed obligation → expected income, with status labels and no false certainty.
5. One compact arithmetic reveal for account balance, protected minimum, local named reserves, goal and confirmed payments.
6. Nearest operation and primary actions below the first answer.

Keep existing Living Card, Financial Horizon / Time Machine, Financial Gravity and locally planned Spending Lock as reachable product concepts. They do not all belong in the first viewport. Spending Lock remains local plan reservation, not a bank-level lock.

## Transition specification for later implementation

- Tap `Состав суммы`: hero amount remains spatially anchored while breakdown opens below; timeline markers keep dates stable. Spring is short and damped. Interactive controls remain tappable.
- Tap a horizon marker: marker selection and corresponding detail share a visual identity; selection does not commit financial data.
- Tab navigation: native navigation transition, not a web-like page wipe; preserve scroll where product conventions allow.
- Reduce Motion: remove translation/scale and stagger; expose the same content/state immediately with a brief opacity or no animation.
- Error/stale/empty states: no value-count-up. Never animate unknown balance into a fabricated number.
