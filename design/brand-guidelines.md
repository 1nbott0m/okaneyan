# okaneyan. brand guidelines

## Brand lockup

- **Exact name:** `okaneyan.` — lowercase, with a final period. Use it for the app display name, wordmark and metadata.
- **Descriptor:** `PERSONAL FINANCE` is a separate supporting line. It never appears inside the app icon.
- **Symbol:** a bold central O sits inside an open orbit; a single rose point anchors the upper-right opening. Preserve the open gaps and centered O exactly as in the supplied Orbit O files.
- **Source files:** the user supplied the approved options in `okaneyan-orbit-o 3/`. Use those exact vectors and icon masters; do not redraw them as a leaf, bean, or S-shaped mark.
- **Placement:** launch, appearance settings, app information, favicon/touch icon and app icon. Avoid repeating the logo on every finance screen.

## Art direction

- Matte charcoal `#111011` / `#151314` is the default interface canvas.
- Warm rose `#EFA8BE` and blush `#F7E9EF` are the accent range. Keep large interface surfaces charcoal or neutral; reserve blush for small highlights and the supplied Light icon.
- Use the supplied Original, Light, Monochrome, and Glass assets exactly. Do not add violet panels or change their orbit geometry.
- App icons are square opaque canvases; iOS applies the platform mask. The supplied source and PNGs do not bake in rounded corners.

## Typography

Use the exact supplied wordmark asset unchanged. Text-only brand labels may use Oswald Variable at weight 500/600, paired with the existing rose period; don't use display typography for longer supporting copy. The spoken/accessibility name remains `okaneyan.`.

## User-selectable icon variants

| Variant | Use |
|---|---|
| Original | Default app icon: rose/graphite symbol on matte black. |
| Light | Dark symbol on a soft pastel-pink field. |
| Monochrome | Neutral symbol and sphere with no chromatic accent. |
| Glass | Same symbol with restrained translucency, edge highlights and soft depth. |

These four choices are app-owned alternate icons. They are not aliases for iOS Home Screen Light, Dark or Tinted display treatments; those system appearance settings remain controlled by iOS.

## Product boundaries

The iOS app remains the product. The browser preview uses the Original mark, favicon, Apple touch icon and a preview-only web manifest with demo data. It does not claim to change the installed iOS app icon.
