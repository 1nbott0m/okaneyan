# Art direction 2026 — controlled money editorial

## Reference lock

**Dominant source:** the product question and actual iOS content hierarchy in MoneyWise/okaneyan: how much is safe to spend before income, and what explains that amount. Reference research informs tone and information framing; no source visual system is copied.

**Preserve:** dark matte canvas `#111011`/`#151314`; official Orbit O; warm rose `#EFA8BE`; soft white text; legible Cyrillic system sans. User-selected CoFo faces remain pending licensed files.

**Borrow:** Morrow's decision-first available-to-spend framing; Finaura's explicit type roles and motion authored per component; Sumeria's plain language. Never adopt crypto trading semantics, unsupported “live” data status, or чужой logo/colors.

**Reject:** display serif substitution, violet, lime, gradients/glows, glass, concentric ornamental rings, generic equal dashboard cards, and decorative handwriting without a product role.

## Three comparable composition studies

- **A / Quiet Horizon (owner-approved; D1 preview label):** one matte Living Card places the safe-to-spend amount before its dated explanation; reserve and obligation context follows as compact lines. One rose timeline mark belongs to the Money Horizon. Keep the reading order clear and vertical on phone.
- **B / Financial Zine:** narrow account of money like a printed ledger: sum, then dated deductions and income horizon as rows divided by rules. Dense but scannable, no faux receipt texture or card stack.
- **C / Precision & Vandalism:** technical alignment, short labels, fine ticks and a compact flow rail; one off-grid rose marker points at next obligation. No decorative animation.

The owner-approved direction is A / Soft Matte Fintech («Тихий горизонт»), recorded in DEC-008. B and C are comparative review hypotheses only and are not product settings. All studies share typography, palette, copy, demo dataset, controls and action semantics; only composition, type density and graphic structure change.

## Motion signature

Money Horizon grows from a fine baseline into a timeline. Expanding the calculation retains the amount in place while deductions enter by row; switching concepts uses a shared amount and cross-grid interpolation; nav uses a short shared-view transition. No ambient pulsing. All gestures interruptible and reduced-motion aware.
