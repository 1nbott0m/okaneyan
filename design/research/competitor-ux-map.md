## Product-specific design consequences

1. The Overview answer should be computed from actual balance and the next confirmed income, then expose its calculation.
2. “Expected” income remains a separate state from confirmed and available money.
3. Reserve can be a local planning allocation only; use wording “защищено в плане”.
4. A scenario purchase belongs to a hypothetical projection; it must not enter Transactions until explicit user action.
5. Gravity should encode the same categories and equations in an editorial register, with a text detail mode that is equally complete; spatial orbit metaphors are removed because categories overlap.

**Coverage:** 11 products/concepts identified in the current map; public product pages, official docs, app-store/case screenshots and one research case were reviewed, not 11 live native applications. Screen-level runtime review remains pending.
