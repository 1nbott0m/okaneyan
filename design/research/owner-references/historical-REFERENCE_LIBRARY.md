## Reading this inventory

- The 50 references do not have equal evidential strength. Only the exact statuses above count.
- Reference screenshots are internal research evidence, not cleared assets. No template files, proprietary images or source code were copied.
- Sources 1–12 contain exact-template misses at Syntaax and Candela; these remain unresolved rather than substituted.
- Native fit means a principle could be evaluated in SwiftUI; it does not mean the source itself is native or accessible.
